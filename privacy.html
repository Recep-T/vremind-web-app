Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

1. Introduction

VRemind ("we", "our", or "us") operates the VRemind learning platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, and password when you register.
  • Profile data: Role (educator, student, parent), organization affiliation.
  • Usage data: Task assignments, progress updates, and messages exchanged within the platform.
  • Google Classroom data: If you connect your Google account, we access your Google Classroom courses, coursework, and submission status solely to display your assignments within VRemind. We do not store, share, or sell this data.
  • Device tokens: Push notification tokens to send task reminders and message alerts.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the VRemind platform.
  • To display your Google Classroom assignments alongside your VRemind tasks.
  • To send push notifications about task deadlines and new messages.
  • To enable communication between educators, students, and parents.
  • To improve our service and fix technical issues.

4. Google Classroom Integration

VRemind integrates with Google Classroom using the following OAuth scopes:

  • classroom.courses.readonly
  • classroom.coursework.me.readonly
  • classroom.student-submissions.me.readonly

We use these permissions only to read and display your course assignments and submission status inside VRemind. We do not modify, delete, or share your Google Classroom data with any third party. Your Google data is fetched in real time and is not permanently stored on our servers.

VRemind's use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Within your organization: Educators can see their students' task progress. Parents can view their child's progress.
  • Service providers: We use Firebase (Google) for database, authentication, and hosting. Data is processed in accordance with Google's privacy terms.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights.

6. Data Security

We use Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database with security rules to protect your data. All data is transmitted over HTTPS. We implement role-based access controls so users can only access data relevant to their role.

7. Children's Privacy

VRemind is designed to be used by students under the supervision of their educators and parents. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Student accounts are created through educator-issued registration codes and are monitored by their organization administrator.

8. Your Rights

  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.
  • You may disconnect Google Classroom access at any time via your Google account settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You may contact us to access or correct your personal data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at vremindapp@gmail.com.
